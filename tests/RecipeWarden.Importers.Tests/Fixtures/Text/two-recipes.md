# Garlic Bread

Crispy and quick.

Serves: 4
Prep time: 10 min

## Ingredients

- 1 baguette
- 4 Tbsp butter, softened
- 2 cloves garlic, minced

## Directions

1. Mix the butter and garlic.
2. **Bake**
   Spread on the bread and bake 10 minutes at 400°F.
