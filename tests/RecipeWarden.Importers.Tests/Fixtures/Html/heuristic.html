Grandma's Biscuits

These are the best biscuits.

Serves 8 · Prep time: 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup cold butter
  • 3/4 cup buttermilk

Directions

  1. Heat the oven to 450°F.
  2. Cut the butter into the flour.
  3. Stir in the buttermilk and bake 12 minutes.

Notes

Freeze unbaked biscuits.
