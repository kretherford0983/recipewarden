# Skillet Mexican Street Corn

This fast to prepare side dish takes on the flavors of elote, a popular Mexican favorite!

Can be a side dish or as a chicken taco topping.

## Prep Time

**Prep:** 5 minutes
**Cook:** 15 minutes
**Total:** 20 Minutes
**Servings:** 8

## Ingredients
### Corn
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 *(16 ounce)* packages frozen corn
- 1/4 cup crumbled cotija cheese (increase to 1/3 - 1/2 cup for taco toping)
- 1/4 cup minced red onion
- 2 tbsp finely chopped cilantro

### Sauce (Side Dish)
- 3 tbsp avacado mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp sour cream
- 2 tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked sweet paprika
- 1/4 tsp chili powder
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1 pinch *(1/16 tsp)* ground coriander

### Sauce (Taco Topping)
- 1/4 cup avacado mayonnaise
- 2.5 tbsp sour cream
- 2.5 tbsp lime juice
- 5/8 tsp ground cumin
- 5/8 tsp smoked sweet paprike
- 5/16 tsp chili powder
- 5/16 tsp salt
- 1/8 tsp ground coriander


## Directions

**Char Corn**
1. Heat a 14-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add olive oil and swirl to coat the bottom of the skillet. Spread frozen corn evenly across the skillet; do not stir. Cook corn for approximately 8 minutes.

**Dressing**
2. Meanwhile, combine mayonnaise, sour cream, lime juice, cumin, paprika, chili powder, salt, and coriander in a large bowl. Set aside

**Check Corn**
3. Check the corn; it should be slightly charred. If not, allow to continue to cooking for 4 more minutes (stirring is fine at this point).

**Coat Corn**
4. Once corn is sufficiently browned/charred, transfer to the bowl with the dressing and toss to coat.

**Finish**
5. Add cotija cheese, red onion, and cilantro and mix until well combined. 
    - *As side dish:* Serve immediately.
    - *As taco topping:* Spoon the corn mixture directly over your cooked chicken inside the tortillas, then use extra cotija cheese to sprinkle over the top of taco.
