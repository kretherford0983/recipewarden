# Kansas Corn Bake (Single Batch)
[Double Batch](#kansas-corn-bake-double-batch)

A classic Midwestern casserole-style side dish—rich, savory, and perfect for potlucks or holiday meals.

---

## Yield

- Serves **6–8 people**
- One **8 × 12 inch** baking dish

---

## Prep Time

- **Prep:** 10 minutes  
- **Bake:** 30–40 minutes  
- **Total:** 40–50 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 1 can whole kernel corn, drained
- 1 can cream-style corn
- 2/3 cup evaporated milk (e.g., Milnot)
- 2 eggs, beaten
- 4 Tbsp margarine, melted
- 2 Tbsp grated onion or minced onion
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup cracker crumbs
- 12 oz shredded cheese

---

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven**  
   Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**.

2. **Mix ingredients**  
   In a large bowl, combine all ingredients and mix until evenly incorporated.

3. **Transfer to baking dish**  
   Pour mixture into a greased **8 × 12 inch** baking dish and spread evenly.

4. **Bake**  
   Bake in the middle of the oven for **30–40 minutes**, or until set and lightly golden on top.

5. **Serve**  
   Allow to cool slightly before serving.

---

## Storage & Reheating

- **Refrigerated:** 3–4 days in an airtight container  
- **Freezing:** Up to 2 months (texture may soften slightly)  
- **Reheat:** Oven at 325°F until warmed through, or microwave individual portions

# Kansas Corn Bake (Double Batch)

A classic Midwestern casserole-style side dish—scaled up for gatherings, potlucks, or leftovers.

---

## Yield

- Serves **12–16 people**
- One **9 × 13 inch** baking dish (thick casserole)

---

## Prep Time

- **Prep:** 15 minutes  
- **Bake:** 65–85 minutes  
- **Total:** 75–100 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 2 cans whole kernel corn, drained
- 2 cans cream-style corn
- 1 1/3 cups (10.7 oz) evaporated milk (e.g., Milnot)
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 8 Tbsp margarine, melted (1 stick)
- 1/4 cup grated or minced onion
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 cups cracker crumbs
- 24 oz shredded cheese

---

## Instructions

1. **Preheat oven**  
   Preheat oven to **375°F (190°C)**.

2. **Prepare ingredients**  
   Using a food processor, process crackers into fine crumbs.  
   Grate the onion.  
   Melt the margarine in the microwave and allow it to cool slightly.

3. **Mix ingredients**  
   In a very large bowl, combine all ingredients and mix until evenly incorporated.

4. **Transfer to baking dish**  
   Pour mixture into a greased **9 × 13 inch** baking dish and spread evenly.

5. **Bake (covered)**  
   Cover loosely with foil and bake in the middle of the oven for **45-60 minutes**, allowing the casserole to cook through without over-browning.

6. **Bake (uncovered)**  
   Remove foil and continue baking for an additional **20-25 minutes**, or until the top is lightly browned and the center is fully set.

7. **Rest and serve**  
   Remove from oven and let rest for **5–10 minutes** before serving. The casserole will continue to firm up as it cools.

---

## Doneness Notes

- Center should be **set, not soupy**
- A knife inserted near the center should come out mostly clean
- Slight jiggle is fine; excess liquid is not

---

## Storage & Reheating

- **Refrigerated:** 3–4 days in airtight containers  
- **Freezing:** Up to 2 months (texture may soften slightly after thawing)  
- **Reheat:**  
  - Oven: 325°F, covered, until warmed through  
  - Microwave: Individual portions
