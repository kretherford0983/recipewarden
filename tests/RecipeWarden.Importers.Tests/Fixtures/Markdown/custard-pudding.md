# Classic Vanilla Custard Pudding

This silky-smooth, rich vanilla custard pudding is a comforting classic dessert that comes together in under 30 minutes on the stovetop.

## Prep Time
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Total:** 25 min (plus chilling time)  
**Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup (40g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 3 cups (720ml) **whole** milk
- 4 large egg yolks, lightly beaten
- 1 tbsp (14g) unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 tsp pure vanilla extract

## Steps

### Combine Dry Ingredients & Milk
1. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the sugar, flour, and salt. 

### Heat Base Mixture
2. Gradually pour in the whole milk while whisking continuously to ensure no lumps remain. Heat over low to medium-low heat until the mixture comes to a gentle simmer.

### Thickening
3. Simmer for 3–5 minutes, stirring constantly, until the pudding thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.

### Temper the Eggs
4. Ladle about 1/2 cup of the hot milk mixture into the beaten egg yolks, whisking vigorously to temper them so they don't scramble. 

### Combine & Cook
5. Slowly pour the tempered egg mixture back into the main pot, whisking constantly. Continue cooking over low heat for another 1–2 minutes until glossy and thick.

### Add Aromatics & Finish
6. Remove the saucepan from the heat. Stir in the butter and vanilla extract until the butter is completely melted and incorporated.

### Chill
7. Pour the pudding into individual serving dishes or a large bowl. Cover with plastic wrap, pressing the film directly onto the surface of the pudding to prevent a skin from forming. Chill in the refrigerator for at least 2–3 hours until set.

# Rich Chocolate Custard Pudding

A deep, decadent chocolate pudding with a velvety finish that beats any store-bought cup.

## Prep Time
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Total:** 25 min (plus chilling time)  
**Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup (40g) all-purpose flour
- 1/3 to 1/2 cup (35–50g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 3 cups (720ml) **whole** milk
- 4 large egg yolks, lightly beaten
- 1 tbsp (14g) unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 tsp pure vanilla extract

## Steps

### Combine Dry Ingredients & Milk
1. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the sugar, flour, cocoa powder, and salt until evenly blended.

### Heat Base Mixture
2. Gradually pour in the whole milk while whisking continuously to break up any dry clumps. Heat over low to medium-low heat until the mixture reaches a gentle simmer.

### Thickening
3. Simmer for 3–5 minutes, whisking constantly, until the pudding thickens and coats the back of a spoon.

### Temper the Eggs
4. Ladle about 1/2 cup of the hot chocolate milk mixture into the beaten egg yolks, whisking vigorously to temper them.

### Combine & Cook
5. Slowly pour the egg mixture back into the saucepan, whisking constantly. Cook over low heat for 1–2 additional minutes until smooth and thick.

### Add Aromatics & Finish
6. Remove from the heat. Stir in the butter and vanilla extract until smooth and glossy.

### Chill
7. Pour into serving dishes or a bowl. Press plastic wrap directly against the surface of the pudding to prevent a skin from forming, and chill in the refrigerator for at least 2–3 hours before serving.

# Fresh Banana Custard Pudding

A classic real-banana custard using fresh mashed banana folded into a silky vanilla base—no artificial flavorings required.

## Prep Time
**Prep:** 12 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Total:** 27 min (plus chilling time)  
**Servings:** 6 servings

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup (40g) all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp fine sea salt
- 3 cups (720ml) **whole** milk
- 4 large egg yolks, lightly beaten
- 1 tbsp (14g) unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup (approx. 2 medium) very ripe bananas, thoroughly mashed or pureed

## Steps

### Combine Dry Ingredients & Milk
1. In a medium saucepan, whisk together the sugar, flour, and salt.

### Heat Base Mixture
2. Gradually pour in the whole milk while whisking continuously to ensure no lumps remain. Heat over low to medium-low heat until the mixture reaches a gentle simmer.

### Thickening
3. Simmer for 3–5 minutes, stirring constantly, until the pudding thickens and coats the back of a spoon.

### Temper the Eggs
4. Ladle about 1/2 cup of the hot milk mixture into the beaten egg yolks, whisking vigorously to temper them.

### Combine & Cook
5. Slowly pour the egg mixture back into the saucepan, whisking constantly. Cook over low heat for 1–2 additional minutes until smooth and thick.

### Add Aromatics & Banana
6. Remove from the heat. Stir in the butter and vanilla extract until smooth. Whisk or fold in the pureed banana until fully integrated into the custard.

### Chill
7. Pour into serving dishes or a bowl. Press plastic wrap directly against the surface of the pudding to prevent a skin from forming, and chill in the refrigerator for at least 2–3 hours before serving.
